Who This Is For

You can explain your patterns.
You just can't break them.

You've done the therapy. Read the books. Built the vision board. You can narrate your wounds with precision, tracing them back to childhood, to a relationship, to a version of yourself you've long since outgrown.

And yet here you are. Still shrinking in moments you vowed you wouldn't. Still choosing the familiar ache over the terrifying unknown. The insight is there. The embodiment hasn't arrived yet. That's exactly where we begin.

About Taylor

I was her.
Now I guide her.

I spent years traveling the world alone. Forty-something countries, countless solitary sunsets, trying to outrun the version of myself I couldn't quite leave behind. I knew my patterns. I'd named them, journaled them, analyzed them to death.

It wasn't until I found somatic work that something finally shifted. I stopped trying to think my way out and started feeling my way through. Not in my mind. In my body. In my bones.

I've since invested deeply in somatic coaching, nervous system work, and emotional alchemy. SPOKEN is the container I wish had existed for me. It's not about fixing you. You're not broken. It's about finally, fully, becoming who you already are.
